# Photographic Credits & Provenance Manifest

This document records the comprehensive photo attribution, provenance tracking, and legal rights verification for all photographic assets utilized outside the interactive Archives section across the WEC 2011–2023 editorial data story.

> **Legal & Attribution Principle**:  
> A **source lead URL**, a **photographer credit**, and an **image copyright** are legally distinct from **permission to reuse an image**. Sourcing an image from a press article or search engine does not grant open reuse rights. Only assets with confirmed open-access licenses (e.g. Creative Commons) or explicit written permissions may be publicly distributed.
>
> **Audit & Replacement Status**:  
> 100% of photographic assets actively rendered on the website (16 of 16) are verified authentic motorsport documentary photographs licensed under open Creative Commons licenses (CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA). All synthetic AI placeholders and unverified candidate assets have been replaced.

## 3. Historical Audit & Filmstrip Re-Curation Record

During initial development, developmental placeholders and candidate leads were investigated and resolved. Subsequently, the 8-photograph hero filmstrip sequence was re-curated to elevate documentary tension and eliminate repetitive single-car GT shots:

1. **Re-curation of Filmstrip Sequence**:
   - Previous sequence relied too heavily on isolated GT cars cornering at similar angles (Aston Martin #98, Porsche 911 RSR #92, Ferrari 488 #51, High Class LMP2 #33).
   - Re-curated to introduce true multiclass warfare: race start in motion (01), high-speed prototype apex (02), LMP2 vs LMP1 nose-to-tail combat (03), prototype overtaking GT car (04), direct GT wheel-to-wheel duel (05), night racing (06), pit crew human intensity (07), and torrential rain rooster-tail spray (08).
   - All 8 assets are verified Creative Commons authentic motorsport photography.

2. **Legacy Synthetic AI Placeholders**:
   - Replaced developmental AI renders with verified authentic documentary photography from United Autosports, John Chapman, David Merrett, and Kevin Decherf.

3. **Proprietary Media Leads (Excluded)**:
   - Dailysportscar 2017 Monza photocall, ACO 2020 official photocall, and DPPI Images 2021 Bahrain start were all identified as All Rights Reserved proprietary media and excluded from the public codebase.
